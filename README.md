# Descrybe Legal Engine

Use Descrybe to research U.S. primary law in Google Antigravity. Search judicial opinions by legal issue or exact phrase, look up statutes and court rules, and check a quotation against the text of an opinion.

## Connect your account

You need a Descrybe account with Legal Engine access, either individually or through your organization.

1. Install the **Descrybe Legal Engine** plugin in Antigravity. To try it from a local checkout, run `agy plugin install .` from this directory.
2. In the Antigravity app, open **Customizations → Installed → MCP Servers**. Select **Authenticate** for the plugin’s Descrybe connection and follow the prompts to sign in to Descrybe and approve access.
3. In a conversation, ask “Use Descrybe to look up 347 U.S. 483. Give me the case name, court, and year, with a link to the opinion.” The result should identify *Brown v. Board of Education*, decided by the U.S. Supreme Court in 1954.

## Research with Descrybe

- **Find authority.** Search by jurisdiction, legal issue, or exact language. Retrieve case details, summaries, and passages, or request a case PDF.
- **Check citations and quotations.** Extract case references from a draft, resolve them to cases, and compare quoted language with the opinion.
- **Trace later decisions.** Check a case’s treatment signal and read the later cases that cite it to see how the court used it.
- **Find laws and rules.** Search statutes, regulations, constitutional provisions, and court rules. Include the jurisdiction and the date relevant to your question.

Open the cited sources to check the text, context, and applicability to your question.
